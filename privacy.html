2. Visiting this website (hosting)

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the site, your browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers, including your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and your browser type. GitHub needs this data to deliver the site and keep it secure, and may store it in server logs. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a secure, working website). GitHub may process data in the USA. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework; details are in GitHub's privacy statement at docs.github.com.

All fonts and scripts this website uses are stored on the same hosting. The only exception is the Forminit form script, which loads only when you send the enquiry form (section 5).

3. Cookies, analytics and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

On your first visit we show a short privacy notice. If you click “Don’t show again”, your browser stores one small entry (asmi-notice, the date of your click) in its local storage, so the notice is not shown again for 180 days. It contains no personal data, is not sent to us and is used only because you asked for it (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG, the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings. If you click “Understood”, nothing is stored.

When you email or call us, we process the details you give us (for example your name, company, email address, phone number, your request and any stock lists or documents you attach) to answer your enquiry and, if it leads to business, to prepare and carry out the contract. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (steps before and for a contract) and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries).

Our business mailbox is provided by Namecheap, Inc., 4600 East Washington Street, Suite 300, Phoenix, AZ 85034, USA (Private Email service). Incoming messages are also forwarded to and managed in a Google Gmail account (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Both providers process the messages on our behalf; data may be transferred to the USA. Google is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

5. The enquiry form on this website

When you use the enquiry form, we process the details you enter (name, email address, company, country, products, quality, tonnage, destination, specification) and any file you attach, such as a stock list. We use them only to answer your enquiry and, if it leads to business, to prepare and carry out the contract. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (steps before a contract at your request) and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries from company representatives). Fields marked "required" are needed to reply to you; everything else is optional.

The form is delivered by Forminit, an EU-hosted form service that processes the submission on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Forminit stores submissions on servers in Ireland (EU) and sends us a notification email. Forminit's script is loaded only when you click "Send enquiry", not when you visit the site. We do not use Forminit's optional AI or tracking features. Details: forminit.com.

Your entries stay in your browser until you click "Send enquiry". If sending fails, you can instead send the enquiry with your own email app; section 4 then applies.

6. How long we keep data

We delete enquiry data when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for. Business letters, offers, contracts and invoices are kept for the periods required by German commercial and tax law (usually 6 or 10 years, § 257 HGB and § 147 AO).

You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit (Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information), Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany.
