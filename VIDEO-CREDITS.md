# ASMI — The steel journey

Original code-generated 3D film, created for Arya Sahel Metal International.

- Eleven stages, 44 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, silent H.264 MP4. A 1280 × 720 version is served on smaller screens.
- All geometry, industrial sets, procedural surfaces, lighting and camera paths are authored in code. No stock footage, stock photographs or downloaded 3D models.
- Rendered using Blender 4.5 LTS; assembled with FFmpeg.
- Illustrative conventional steelmaking and through-ICD delivery route. No depiction establishes ownership, a supplier relationship, an actual shipment, guaranteed routing or capacity. ASMI is a trader.
- Inland rail to ICD Kamalapur and clearance there apply only to appropriately booked cargo.
- The previously used Mixkit clips are no longer included in the film.

Reproducible scene source and assembly scripts are retained in the ASMI project workspace.
