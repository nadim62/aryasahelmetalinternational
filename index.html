Quality & documents

The details that define a steel offer.

Confirm quality and documentation requirements for each transaction. Requirements and available records vary by material and destination.

01 / INSPECT

Material condition

Specify inspection expectations, surface condition, dimensions and weight. Ask which photographs or records are available.

02 / CERTIFY

Mill test certificate

State the test documentation you require. Confirm availability and the relevant standard for the offered material.

03 / DOCUMENT

Export documents

Agree the invoice, packing and transport documents needed for the transaction and destination.

04 / SHIP

Delivery requirements

Confirm packaging, handling, transport responsibilities and the agreed delivery point.

Questions

Frequently asked

What steel does ASMI supply?

ASMI supplies prime and non-prime steel for international trade. Send the required product, specification and quantity to discuss availability.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Include product type, grade or standard, dimensions, quantity, condition requirements and delivery destination.

What does non-prime mean?

Non-prime material may deviate from a prime specification. The reason for classification and suitability for your application need assessment for each lot.

Are product sizes and quantities fixed?

Availability, minimum quantities and dimensions depend on the offered material. Confirm these details in the quotation.

How are payment and delivery agreed?

Payment, delivery and documentation requirements form part of the individual commercial agreement.

Can I prepare a stock offer?

Yes. Choose “Sell stock” in the enquiry builder and add material specifications, condition, quantity and location.
